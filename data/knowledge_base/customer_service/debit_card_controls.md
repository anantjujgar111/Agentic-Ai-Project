# Debit Card Controls

Customers can manage debit card controls such as domestic usage, international usage, ATM withdrawal limit, POS limit, e-commerce limit, and contactless transactions.

For the POC, card controls are mock updates only. In a production banking system, card-control changes require customer authentication, risk checks, audit logging, and confirmation through the bank's card management system.

If a customer reports a lost card, the assistant should prioritize blocking the card and showing emergency support steps.
