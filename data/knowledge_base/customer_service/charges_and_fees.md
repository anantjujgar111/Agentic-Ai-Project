# Charges And Fees

This synthetic article is for the POC and does not represent live HDFC Bank charges.

## Debit card annual fee

The sample Debit Platinum card has an illustrative annual maintenance fee of Rs. 499. Premium account variants may receive waivers based on segment, balance, salary relationship, or card program rules.

## Failed auto-debit

If an auto-debit instruction fails because of insufficient funds, a penalty may apply. The chatbot should explain the transaction narration and suggest keeping funds before the due date.

## Fund transfer charges

UPI is usually preferred for low-value instant payments. IMPS is useful for instant medium-value transfers. NEFT is suitable for scheduled non-urgent transfers. RTGS is suitable for high-value same-day transfers.

## Safe response rule

The assistant must not confirm real money movement unless a real core banking API returns a successful transaction reference.
