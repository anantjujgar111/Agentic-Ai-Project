# Savings Account FAQ

This synthetic knowledge article describes common savings account queries for the banking assistant POC.

## Minimum balance

Savings Max customers should maintain an average monthly balance of Rs. 10,000 in metro branches. Preferred customers may have segment-specific waivers. The assistant must tell users that final eligibility depends on the bank's official schedule of charges.

## Interest credit

Savings account interest is calculated on the daily closing balance and credited quarterly. Interest rates can change based on bank policy and regulatory guidance.

## Account statement

Customers can request monthly, quarterly, or custom-period statements through mobile banking, net banking, branch, or relationship manager support.

## KYC

KYC refresh may require PAN, Aadhaar, address proof, recent photograph, and in-person verification depending on the customer's risk category.
