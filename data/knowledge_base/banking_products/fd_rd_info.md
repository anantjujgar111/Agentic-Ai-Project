# Fixed Deposit And Recurring Deposit Info

Fixed Deposits allow customers to place a lump sum for a fixed tenure at a contracted interest rate. Recurring Deposits allow customers to save a fixed monthly amount for a chosen tenure.

## FD management

The assistant can show existing FD details, explain maturity date, and simulate creating a new FD in the POC. Production FD creation requires source account selection, nominee handling, tax instructions, and final confirmation.

## RD management

The assistant can suggest RD monthly amounts for goals. Production RD setup requires standing instruction setup and confirmation.

## Goal linkage

For short-term goals, the assistant may suggest a recurring deposit or liquid savings bucket. For long-term goals, it must include a disclaimer and should not give regulated investment advice without appropriate controls.
